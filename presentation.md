# 🧠 NeuralData
### The AI-Powered Data Intelligence Agent

*Hackathon 2026 Pitch Deck*

---

## 🛑 The Data Chaos Problem
Enterprise data is broken.

- **Fragmented:** Spread across Postgres, Snowflake, MongoDB, and S3.
- **Undocumented:** No one knows what `customer_ltv_v2_final` means.
- **Unsecure:** Sensitive PII (SSNs, emails) is scattered unknowingly.
- **The Result:** <span class="highlight">Engineers spend 40% of their time finding and verifying data</span> instead of building.

---

## ✨ Our Solution: NeuralData
A zero-setup **Data Intelligence Engine** that automatically connects to your silos, profiles schemas, and creates a unified Knowledge Layer.

1. **Connect:** Securely read schemas via automated pipelines.
2. **Profile:** ML-driven PII Named Entity Recognition.
3. **Score:** Real-time SLA & Data Trust scoring.
4. **Chat:** Ask plain-language questions to the AI Assistant.
